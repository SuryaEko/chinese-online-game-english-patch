刮刮乐的中奖几率通常在30%~50%之间\n\r\n‌中奖几率因款式和设计而异‌,不同款式、发行量及具体设计的刮刮乐产品中奖率可能达到40%~50%，意味着在大多数情况下，购买的刮刮乐中至少有一半的机会能中奖。不过也有刮刮乐的中奖率在30%左右。但正常整本刮刮乐的中奖几率大约在30%到45%左右。\r\n\r\n‌区分中奖率和返奖率‌：中奖率是指符合中奖条件的注数占总注数的比例，而返奖率则是指用来返还彩民的奖金在销售额中所占的比例。经销商在宣传时可能会提到较高的返奖率（如65%），但这并不等同于中奖率。实际上，排除大奖和超级大奖的影响后，真正的返奖率通常也就是50%左右。=The winning rate of scratch cards is usually between 30%~50%\n\nWinning rates vary by style and design. For different styles, print runs and designs, scratch cards may reach 40%~50%, meaning in most cases at least half of the cards you buy have a chance to win. Some scratch cards are around 30%. But the winning rate for a whole book of scratch cards is generally about 30% to 45%.\n\nDistinguish winning rate from payout rate: the winning rate is the proportion of winning tickets among all tickets, while the payout rate is the proportion of sales returned as prizes. Dealers may advertise a higher payout rate (such as 65%), but this is not the same as the winning rate. In fact, excluding big and super prizes, the real payout rate is usually about 50%.
